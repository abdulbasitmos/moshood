Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 2-3 months in advance for large events like weddings. For smaller events, 2-4 weeks notice is usually sufficient. However, we can accommodate last-minute bookings subject to availability.

Can I customize the menu?

Absolutely! We offer fully customizable menus to suit your preferences, dietary requirements, and cultural traditions. Our chefs will work with you to create the perfect menu for your event.

Are all your dishes halal certified?

Yes, all our ingredients and preparation processes are halal-certified. We understand the importance of halal food to our Muslim clients and ensure strict compliance with halal standards.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made 30 days before the event receive a full refund. Cancellations made 15-29 days before receive 50% refund. Cancellations within 14 days are non-refundable. We recommend event insurance for protection.

Do you provide equipment and setup?

Yes! All packages include complete equipment, table setup, and service staff. Premium and Luxury packages also include decoration. We handle everything from setup to cleanup.

Can you cater to dietary restrictions?

Certainly! We can accommodate vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and other dietary requirements. Please inform us during booking so we can prepare suitable options.
