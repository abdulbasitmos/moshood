About Us

Our Story

A journey of culinary excellence and passion

15 Years of Excellence

Founded in 2009, Yusmad Catering Services began with a simple vision: to create extraordinary culinary experiences that bring people together. What started as a small family business has grown into one of Malaysia's most trusted luxury catering companies.

Our founder, Chef Yusmad, brought together a passion for traditional Malay cuisine with modern culinary techniques, creating a unique blend that has become our signature style. Over the years, we've had the honor of catering thousands of weddings, corporate events, and special celebrations.

Today, we continue to uphold our commitment to excellence, using only the finest ingredients and employing skilled chefs who share our passion for creating memorable dining experiences.

Mission & Vision

Our guiding principles and aspirations

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional catering services that exceed our clients' expectations through culinary excellence, impeccable service, and attention to detail. We strive to make every event a memorable celebration by combining traditional flavors with innovative presentation.

Our Vision

To be recognized as Malaysia's premier luxury catering service, setting industry standards for quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction. We envision a future where Yusmad Catering becomes synonymous with excellence in event catering across Southeast Asia.
